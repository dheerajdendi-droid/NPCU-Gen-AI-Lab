## 1. Complaint Recognition

This section defines how North Pennine Community Credit Union (NPCU) applies complaint recognition in day-to-day operations. The control intent is to make decisions repeatable, evidenced and capable of independent review.

Operational teams must record enough evidence for a reviewer to reconstruct the decision without relying on the memory of the original colleague. Where a system rule and a documented policy appear to conflict, the current approved policy takes precedence and the discrepancy must be raised as a change or incident.

Managers are expected to monitor both individual exceptions and emerging patterns. A single exception may be reasonable; a recurring exception can indicate a control design problem, training issue, configuration defect or change in member behaviour.

### Key controls

- **1.1** A complaint is any expression of dissatisfaction where a response or resolution is expected, regardless of the channel used.
- **1.2** Colleagues must not require the member to use specific words or a formal form before recognising a complaint.
- **1.3** Complaints arising during arrears conversations must be recorded while appropriate account management continues.
